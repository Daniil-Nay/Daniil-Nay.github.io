Generated by thinkingmachines/Inkling-Small Streaming audio-visual target speaker extraction (AVTSE) is essential for latency-sensitive applications. First, standard benchmarks fix the number of speakers at two and ignore the near-/far-field interference, reverberation, and sudden noise of real scenes. Code and demo are available at https://cslikai.cn/Falcon/.
